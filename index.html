About

Hello, I’m Hwan Heo. I work where generative models, systems, and graphics meet.

At NC AI, I lead research and engineering for the VARCO 3D, developing production 3D AI systems across large-scale generative model training, inference optimization, and graphics algorithms.

When I'm away from CUDA kernels and mesh pipelines, I'm usually playing guitar, making music, or following an unexpected STEM rabbit hole.
