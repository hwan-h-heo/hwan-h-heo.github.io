# Generated Manim figures

These nine 720p H.264 clips and WebP posters are generated by the separate
`quad_remesh_blog` ManimCE project. `manifest.json` records their sizes, durations,
and scene names. Render caches and intermediate clips are not deployed.

The article uses enlarged static variants for pairing, charts/seams, the cross
field's local frame, and integer transitions. The remaining five examples retain
animation because motion or step order helps explain them. The original clips
and posters remain available here.

The four images in `stills/` are native Manim renders of the completed source
scenes, with a tighter camera frame and without video-only titles, narration, or
context insets. They are not captures from the encoded videos. Their WebP exports
are 1600px wide at quality 88, totaling about 191 KiB. Integer transitions also
have an 800px-wide stacked variant for screens up to 600px, adding about 26 KiB.
The browser selects one layout without downloading both. `stills/manifest.json`
records dimensions, sizes, source scene names, and source-file hashes.

Regenerate them from the site repository with the original project's Python
environment:

```sh
~/Documents/manim_skill/.venv/bin/python scripts/quad-remesh-stills.py
```

Set `QUAD_REMESH_MANIM_SOURCE` if the source project is checked out elsewhere.
`--scene CarrierStill` renders just one variant; `--width` and `--quality` adjust
the export. `--export-only` re-encodes existing native PNG renders in the work
directory. Temporary renders stay under `_workspace/`, outside deployed assets.

They are synthetic teaching examples. The chart and integer-fixing clips use a
computed 32-triangle patch, separate from the article's armadillo captures.
The carrier figure focuses on three adjacent faces from this patch, with an
isometric local-coordinate view of the highlighted triangle and matching A/B/C
vertex labels. The pairing
comparison highlights opposite-edge strips in a separately constructed quad mesh.
The article keeps detailed mathematical conditions in expandable notes.
